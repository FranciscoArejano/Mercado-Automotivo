Chevy Sonic Crossover To Begin Production Soon
By Deivis Centeno
February 4, 2026 5:04 am
Facebook
Twitter
LinkedIn
Pinterest
Email
Print
1
General Motors is preparing to start regular production of the upcoming Chevy Sonic crossover in Brazil, where the final touches are being put on the main factory for Chevrolet‘s all-new entry-level utility vehicle.
The automaker’s Brazilian subsidiary announced that it will soon begin production of the first units of the upcoming Chevy Sonic crossover at the GM Gravataí plant, which has received significant upgrades and the latest smart manufacturing technologies for the imminent assembly of the Bowtie brand’s new model. The company launched a web series on social media dedicated to the innovations of the Sonic crossover and has begun the countdown to its unveiling.
“The General Motors factory in Gravataí, Rio Grande do Sul, is already preparing for the production of the Chevrolet Sonic with advanced smart factory processes,” said GM Brazil. “Automation, integrated digital systems, expanded use of artificial intelligence in process controls, and real-time monitoring are already part of the operation, increasing efficiency, precision, quality control, and safety on the production line,” the company added.
In this regard, the automaker announced that the GM Gravataí plant has increased its level of technology and digitalization in preparation for the imminent start of production of the Chevy Sonic crossover – which has been anticipated as an “unprecedented and super-technological” model. The plant underwent a complete transformation with the latest digital manufacturing solutions, which, according to GM Brazil, have turned it into a smart factory, solidifying its position as one of the most advanced automotive plants in the region.
The upgrades to the Gravataí complex for manufacturing the Chevy Sonic crossover include nearly 1,000 robots, artificial intelligence solutions, and multiple integrated systems that improve every manufacturing process at the plant – known as the birthplace of the successful Chevy Onix. As such, the plant is now more efficient and optimized to ensure higher quality in its vehicles, as well as in the operation of its digital systems.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by General Motors Brasil (@generalmotorsbrasil)
The upcoming Chevy Sonic crossover is expected to be officially unveiled and begin regular production in the coming weeks at GM’s Gravataí plant in Brazil, which will supply the country and other markets in the region where the new model will be introduced. Using the same platform and general body structure as the Onix hatchback, the Sonic crossover will enter the utility vehicle segment, targeting the most affordable and popular cars in South America.
